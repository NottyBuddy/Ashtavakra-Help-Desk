Team Lead Password Recovery

Existing Team Lead account ka password reset karein. Is process se koi naya Admin account create nahi hoga.

Recovery key wahi secret hai jo pehle Cloudflare Pages ke Settings → Variables and Secrets mein ADMIN_RECOVERY_TOKEN naam se save ki thi. Is key ya password ko kisi ke saath share na karein. Reset ke baad existing Team Lead ke sabhi sessions sign out ho jayenge.
